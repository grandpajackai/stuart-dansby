This notice describes the features of this website and how they handle your information.

Inquiry and invitation drafts

Form details remain in the current page while you prepare your draft. This site does not send them to a database or automatically email them. When you choose “Open in email app,” your email application receives a draft. You decide whether to send it. When you choose “Copy email draft,” the draft is copied to your device’s clipboard.

Sending an email shares its contents with Taking the Fight and the email services used to deliver it. Please include only information needed to discuss your event or seminar invitation, and avoid sensitive personal details.

Reflection activities

Any reflection you enter in the seminar page is handled in your browser. The website does not upload or store it. Downloading, printing, or copying a reflection places it on your own device.

Video and external websites

YouTube video players load only after you choose to play a video. They use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed domain. Playing a video or following an external link allows that service to handle information according to its own policies. See Google’s privacy policy.

Hosting and analytics

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. No advertising pixels, marketing trackers, site analytics, or tracking cookies have been added. GitHub may process technical information necessary to provide its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about information you send to Taking the Fight, contact info@takingthefight.com.

The curriculum

Five lessons. Five tools.

Starts with a welcome from Stuart at his desk: why a fighter is teaching business and life, and how to get the most from the course.

01
Lesson 1

Perspective

“Stress is what happens to you. Challenge is what you decide to do about it.”

At 62, the state licensing body tried to keep Stuart out of his title fight with test after test. He turned the stress into a challenge.

Tool: Name It. Perspective-ize It. F.A.W. (Find A Way). Build the Checklist.

18–20 min
02
Lesson 2

Discipline

“Successful people do what unsuccessful people don’t want to do.”

Training twice a day for his first fight in a ring while running a major budget as a new Director of Sales Operations.

Tool: Find the Fight. Weigh It. Take It First. Sign It.

19–21 min
03
Lesson 3

Your Real Competition Is You

“Your real competition is not the person across from you. It is who you were yesterday.”

A world champion’s challenge, the worst sparring day of camp, and the next morning across from a top-ranked UFC middleweight.

Tool: Pick Your Opponent. Watch the Film. Corner Talk. Win the Round.

21–23 min
04
Lesson 4

Servant Leadership & the Power of the Team

“Good people will put more pressure on themselves than you ever can. So don’t put pressure on them.”

Two rival coaches who only came together to corner Stuart, and a VP who owned a budget miss with “we.”

Tool: Build Your Corner. Resource Them. Listen Without Ego. Let Them Own the Fix.

18–20 min
05
Lesson 5

Championship Performance

“Three things happen every day: excuses, apologies and results. Only one of them matters.”

The title fight at 62: moved to the main event, walking out in complete peace, and every pillar inside one night.

Tool: The Daily Fight Card: a three-minute morning routine.

14–16 min
